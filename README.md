# Customer Purchase Behavior & Sales Insights Dashboard

**Excel Learning Project | Customer Analytics & Data Visualization**

## Summary:

Customer Purchase Behavior & Sales Insights Dashboard is an Excel-based learning project focused on analyzing customer purchasing patterns using demographic, income, occupation, education, region, age, and commute-distance data.

An interactive dashboard was created using PivotTables, PivotCharts, and Slicers to explore bike purchase behavior and identify customer segments with higher purchase rates. The project demonstrates practical skills in Excel data analysis, customer segmentation, data visualization, and business insight generation.

## Business Problem

A bike sales business wants to better understand which customer characteristics are associated with bike purchase decisions. The available customer data contains demographic, financial, occupational, geographic and commuting information, but the business needs a simple analytical view to identify customer segments with stronger purchase potential.

The key business challenge is to determine where the business should focus its marketing and sales efforts rather than treating all customers in the same way.

## Business Questions

The analysis focuses on the following questions:

- **What is the overall bike purchase situation among the customers?**
- **Which age group has the strongest bike purchase behavior?**
- **Does commute distance appear to influence bike purchase decisions?**
- **Which region has the highest proportion of bike purchasers?**
- **How does average income differ between customers who purchased and did not purchase a bike?**
- **Does the relationship between income and purchase behavior differ by gender?**
- **Which customer characteristics appear most useful for identifying potential bike buyers?**
- **What customer segments should the business prioritize for targeted marketing?**

## Methodology

The project followed a structured Excel-based workflow: **data preparation → cleaning → transformation → analysis → visualization → dashboard development**.

- Created separate Working, PivotTable, and Dashboard sheets to organize the analysis.
- Removed duplicate records and standardized categorical values for readability.
- Transformed scattered age values into meaningful Age Groups for comparative analysis.
- Created PivotTables to analyze bike purchases across demographic, income, occupation, education, commute, and regional attributes.
- Developed PivotCharts based on the key business questions.
- Consolidated the visualizations into an interactive Excel Dashboard using Slicers and consistent formatting.
- Interpreted the dashboard results to identify customer purchase patterns and potential business opportunities.

## Skills Used

**Microsoft Excel:** Data cleaning, duplicate removal, data standardization, data transformation, PivotTables, PivotCharts, Slicers, dashboard development, data visualization, formatting.

**Data Analysis:** Exploratory data analysis (EDA), customer segmentation, purchase behavior analysis, comparative analysis, demographic analysis, pattern identification, business insight generation.

**Business Analytics:** Customer analytics, sales analysis, marketing insights, customer targeting, business recommendations, data-driven decision support.

## Result Obtained

The dashboard analysis of **1,000 customer records** identified clear differences in bike purchase behavior across age, commute distance, region, and income.

- **481 customers purchased a bike, while 519 did not, resulting in an overall purchase rate of 48.1%.**
- **Middle Age customers recorded the highest purchase rate at approximately 54.6%,** compared with 35.5% for Adolescents and 31.2% for the Old Age group.
- **Customers with a 2–5 mile commute showed the highest purchase rate at approximately 58.6%.**
- **The Pacific region recorded the highest purchase rate at approximately 58.9%,** compared with 49.3% in Europe and 43.3% in North America.
- **Customers who purchased bikes had a higher average income ($57,963)** than customers who did not ($54,875).

## Business Recommendations

### 1. **Prioritize Middle Age Customers**

Focus targeted marketing campaigns on the Middle Age segment, which demonstrates the strongest observed purchase behavior.

### 2. **Target 2–5 Mile Commuters**

Develop campaigns specifically for customers with 2–5 mile commutes, emphasizing convenience, health benefits, cost savings and everyday mobility.

### 3. **Increase Focus on the Pacific Region**

The Pacific region has the highest observed purchase rate. The business should consider region-specific promotions, campaigns and customer acquisition activities in this market.

### 4. **Use Income as a Supporting Segmentation Variable**

Customers who purchased bikes had a higher average income than non-purchasers. Income can therefore be considered alongside other customer characteristics when developing targeted campaigns.

### 5. **Use Multi-Dimensional Customer Segmentation**

Rather than targeting customers using a single characteristic, combine:

**Age + Commute Distance + Region + Income**

For example, **Middle Age + 2–5 Mile Commute + Pacific Region** could be evaluated as a high-potential segment.
